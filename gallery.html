🌟 Welcome to Our Surreal Digital World! 🌟

Step into a realm where music meets interactive art! This isn't just a website— it's a gateway to an alternative digital dimension where reality bends and creativity knows no bounds. Explore our musical universe and embark on extraordinary adventures through sound and pixels.

GOLGI STUDIOS presents a unique multimedia experience that challenges conventional boundaries between music, gaming, and digital art. Our project is an exploration of surreal aesthetics inspired by early internet culture, retro gaming, and experimental electronic music.

🎵 About The Album 🎵

Our latest musical creation transcends traditional boundaries, blending electronic soundscapes with experimental melodies. Each track is carefully crafted to transport you to different dimensions of consciousness, perfectly complementing the interactive game experience that brings these sonic worlds to life.

The album features a unique fusion of:
• Retro-futuristic electronic beats
• Ambient soundscapes inspired by digital environments
• Experimental compositions that mirror the game's surreal progression
• Interactive audio elements that respond to gameplay
• Original sound design created specifically for our virtual world

🎮 About The Game 🎮

Journey through impossible landscapes in our companion game - "Juego Numero 1, Version 0.2". Navigate surreal environments filled with floating chests, mysterious teleportation devices, and ever-changing lighting that responds to your presence. This isn't just a game; it's an interactive music video brought to life!

Game Features:
• Fully 3D exploration in surreal, colorful environments
• Dynamic lighting system that creates atmospheric experiences
• Interactive chest collection mechanics with surprises
• Multiple levels with unique teleportation systems
• Original character controller with jumping and movement physics
• Synchronized audio-visual experience with the album
• Desktop and mobile controls for accessibility

🎨 Visual Art & Aesthetics 🎨

Our visual direction draws heavily from early 2000s web design, Y2K aesthetics, and the charming "amateur" look of personal websites from the internet's golden age. We've embraced the colorful, unpolished beauty of Geocities pages, Beanie Baby websites, and retro game interfaces.

Visual Elements Include:
• Bright, clashing color palettes inspired by rainbow gradients
• Intentionally "amateur" design choices that feel nostalgic
• Animated backgrounds and interactive cursor effects
• Comic Sans and other "unprofessional" fonts used deliberately
• Floating elements and excessive animations
• 3D models with intentionally simple, colorful textures
• Screenshots and artwork showcasing the game's surreal environments

⚙️ Technical Information ⚙️

The game is built using modern web technologies to ensure broad accessibility while maintaining the retro aesthetic. The website itself is a testament to our commitment to the early internet aesthetic, complete with purposefully "unprofessional" design choices.

Technologies Used:
• Three.js for 3D graphics and WebGL rendering
• Web Audio API for interactive sound design
• Responsive CSS with deliberate retro styling
• Progressive enhancement for mobile devices
• Custom physics simulation for character movement
• GLTF/GLB 3D model loading and animation

⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNING ⚠️

This experience contains rapidly flashing lights, bright colors, and intense visual effects. Not recommended for people with epilepsy or those who are easily triggered by strobing visuals. Please take regular breaks and enjoy responsibly!
